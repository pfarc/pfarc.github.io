Delete Your Account - Cüzdanım App

Thank you for using Cüzdanım - Budget App. We respect your privacy and want to make it easy for you to delete your data.

Important: All your app data is stored in your own Google Drive account. We do not store or access any of your personal data on our servers.

Steps to Delete Your Backup Data

  1. Open the Cüzdanım app on your device.
  2. Go to Settings → Google Drive Backup.
  3. Disconnect your Google Drive account from the app.
  4. Open your Google Drive account and locate the folder named "Cüzdanım Backup".
  5. Delete the folder to permanently remove all backup files.
